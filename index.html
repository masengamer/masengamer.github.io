A.N.G.E.L

Automatic gaming clip editor

A.N.G.E.L is a personal tool that finds popular gaming clips on Twitch, edits them into vertical short videos (captions, streamer credit, background music) and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

How it uses Google

The app asks for one permission: upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload). It uses this only to upload the short videos it creates. It cannot read, edit or delete your existing videos, and it does not access any other Google data.
