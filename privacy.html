Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

A.N.G.E.L ("the app") is a personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's computer. This policy explains what data it handles.

Google user data

  • What we access: only the permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload).
  • How we use it: solely to upload short videos the app has created, with the title, description and tags the app generates.
  • Storage: the access token Google issues is stored only in a local file on the computer running the app. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Deletion: you can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the local token file removes it from the computer.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are made through the YouTube API Services and are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Other data

The app downloads public Twitch clips to edit them. It does not collect personal information about viewers or streamers and uses no analytics or cookies.
